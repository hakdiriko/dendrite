dendrite · teach the fly

Play the fly's moves on dendrite's own echo, over a loop. Every move you make, and every moment you leave alone, is what the fly will learn from. A take starts with Play and is saved when you stop.

How it works

The sound is dendrite's own echo, the same C++ code as the plug-in, compiled to WebAssembly and running in your browser. The itch is off. The keys play the fly's body: the moves the fly makes in 2.4.3, with its choices fixed (rush runs double time for a beat, dive stays down for a half note). The log keeps every key at the exact sample it took hold, so each take can be rendered again offline and compared. Takes are saved to this page's own store, where Claude reads them.

Your own loops: a WAV plays exactly; other formats are converted to 48 kHz. Keep the files in C:\Claude-Playground\dendrite\loops so the teaching run can find them by name.
