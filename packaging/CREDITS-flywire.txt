The fly
-------
dendrite's itch is driven by a model of part of a real fruit fly's brain
(Drosophila melanogaster): 11,705 neurons and their connections, cut from the
FlyWire whole-brain connectome and converted into a compact table inside the
plug-in. The neurons are simulated as in Shiu et al. 2024, with learning,
moods and the link to the echo added for dendrite.

Connectome data: FlyWire Consortium, public release 783.
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International
  (CC BY-NC 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
  Changes: a subset of neurons and synapses was selected, weighted and
  re-encoded for real-time use; nothing else of the data is distributed.
  https://flywire.ai  -  https://codex.flywire.ai

  Dorkenwald, S. et al. Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain.
    Nature 634, 124-138 (2024).
  Schlegel, P. et al. Whole-brain annotation and multi-connectome cell
    typing of Drosophila. Nature 634, 139-152 (2024).
  Zheng, Z., Lauritzen, J. S. et al. A complete electron microscopy volume
    of the brain of adult Drosophila melanogaster. Cell 174, 730-743 (2018).

Brain model: Shiu, P. K. et al. A Drosophila computational brain model
  reveals sensorimotor processing. Nature 634, 210-219 (2024).
  Code: github.com/philshiu/Drosophila_brain_model (MIT licence).

The fly is a model inspired by these data. It is not a scientific
instrument, and FlyWire and the authors above do not endorse dendrite.
