dendrite - install on macOS (11 or newer, Apple Silicon and Intel)
=================================================================

Easiest: the installer (dendrite-x.y.z-macos.pkg)
1. Quit your DAW and double-click the .pkg.
2. macOS says it can't check the developer. Click Done (or OK), open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway"
   next to the dendrite message, then confirm. This happens once.
3. Click through the installer. dendrite goes to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins
   (VST3 and Audio Unit).
4. Ableton Live: Live > Settings > Plug-Ins, turn on "Use Audio Units v2"
   and/or "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders", click Rescan.

By hand (without the installer)
1. Copy dendrite.vst3 to      ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   Copy dendrite.component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
2. Open Terminal and paste this one line, so macOS lets the copies load:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/dendrite.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/dendrite.component

3. Rescan in your DAW as above.
