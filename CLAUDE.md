# CLAUDE.md

**dendrite** (always lowercase; repo `knit`; called viber until 0.2.0): a dub echo that grows around your head. A zero-setup VST3/AU audio effect, C++/JUCE, for Hakan.

Read before working:
- `docs/PROJECT_BRIEF.md`: vision, principles, roadmap, autonomy rules. Read at the start of every session.
- `docs/DSP_PLAYBOOK.md`: DSP mechanisms, real-time rules, quality checklist. Read before touching any DSP code.
- Use the `audio-plugin-dev` skill for all plug-in code.

Layout:
- `Source/`: `Processor` (parameters, host time, state) and `Editor` (the window).
  - `Source/dsp/`: `Echo` (the mid/side dub echo: tape, breathing, throw, space), `Itch` (the listener and the five heads), `Fly` (the itch since 2.1: a real fly's brain, since 3.1 13,065 neurons of Janelia's male CNS (until 3.0 FlyWire's female), with its mushroom body, smell, restless neurons, moods and learning since 2.2; data generated by `tools/fly/export.py` into `Source/flies/<fly>/`), `Creature` (2.0a's 32-neuron mind, still the itch's memory; plan in `docs/CREATURE.md`), `Hand` (2.1 sketch, off), `Engine` (clock, itch frames, dry/wet, safety limiter), `Tools.h` (primitives).
  - `Source/keep/`: `Keep` (the last minute on two tapes, the diary of marks, bar-aligned WAV takes; the creature's first memory).
  - `Source/ui/` (3.0, "the preparation": an electrophysiology rig, the fly is the specimen; black, five greys, Fragment Mono, hairlines; every number on screen comes from real state): `Rig` (tones, fonts, hairlines, look and feel), `Header` (specimen data, state, clock, rate/block), `Specimen` (the cut's neurons at their atlas places, exposure, scars, the electrode = feed/space, press = throw, hover = the neuron's identity in its atlas; since 3.2 a rail: imaging method, this session's 2-DG brain with its time-lapse, the goniometer turning the brain in 3D), `Instruments` (the readout column), `Log` (events; keep's drag into Live), `Trace` (3.3, under the specimen: your sound, the fly's reflexes, your echo bent white, tape pitch, the last 8 bars), `Settings` (itch selector, heads, dry, output), `HostFocus` (Live's focus before a drag on Windows).
  - `Source/rig/`: `Recording` (message-thread recorder: traces, exposure and scars saved in the "rig" state child, raster, log, mood history; since 3.2 the imaging memories: calcium, frame, photons, session-only 2-DG uptake and its time-lapse, and the view: method, yaw, pitch), `Timeline` (3.3: what the fly did to your sound, fed one frame per engine call through a lock-free FIFO in the processor's display, the last minute in 10 ms bins plus reflexes, grooms and throws; not saved), `Depth.h` (each neuron's depth, generated per fly by `tools/rig/depth.py`, place proven against FlyData), `Identities.h` (a wrapper for the fly's own `Identities`: FlyWire's by `tools/rig/identities.py`, match proven by identical wiring; the male's by `tools/fly/export.py`).
- `Source/flies/`: one folder per fly, picked by CMake `-DDENDRITE_FLY=malecns|flywire`: `malecns` (Janelia male CNS v1.0, CC BY; the default since 3.1, Hakan's choice 2026-11-23) and `flywire` (FlyWire 783, CC BY-NC: never ship it for sale; build it with `-DDENDRITE_FLY=flywire`, renderer with `-DDENDRITE_FLY_FLYWIRE=1` and `Source/flies/flywire/FlyData.cpp`). Each has `FlyData`, `Atlas.h` (calibration and names), `Identities` and `Depth`. CI keeps a linux-flywire build so it still compiles. Credits: `packaging/CREDITS.txt` (male), `packaging/CREDITS-flywire.txt`.
- `Resources/fonts/`: Fragment Mono (OFL), embedded as binary data.
- `docs/research/imaging/`: the "dendrite, imaged" research page (2026-11-21) and its renders, with Hakan's picks; keep it.
- `docs/manual/`: the one-page manual (`dendrite-manual.html` → `dendrite-manual.pdf` via `node docs/manual/render_manual.js`; CI ships the PDF next to the plug-in).
- `tools/keeptest/`: keep tested headless with a fake transport (`-DDENDRITE_TOOLS=ON`, `dendrite_keeptest loop.wav outdir`).
- `tools/fly/`: the whole fly brain offline (numpy), the cut and the export.
- `tools/render/`: offline renderer without JUCE (`g++ -O2 -std=c++20 -I Source tools/render/render.cpp Source/dsp/*.cpp Source/flies/malecns/FlyData.cpp -o build/render`); `tools/snapshot/`: renders the window to PNG (`-DDENDRITE_TOOLS=ON`, run under `xvfb-run`; `DENDRITE_PLAY=bpm` fakes a playing host).
- `tools/nulltest/`: the processor as a host runs it, output as raw floats; build it on two commits and compare (`-DDENDRITE_TOOLS=ON`, `dendrite_nulltest in.wav out.f32 48000 512 ...`).
- `tools/discovery_audit.py`: sweeps a built VST3 for blow-ups, silence, dead zones and level jumps.
- `.github/workflows/build.yml`: every push builds Windows and Linux; a manual run (workflow_dispatch) adds macOS (VST3 + AU). pluginval at strictness 10 everywhere, the audit on Linux.
- `DECISIONS.md` (one line per decision, plus why) and `CHANGELOG.md`: keep both current.

Build locally (Linux needs the apt packages listed in the workflow):

```
cmake -B build -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release
cmake --build build --config Release
python3 tools/discovery_audit.py build/dendrite_artefacts/Release/VST3/dendrite.vst3
```

Done means: CI green on all three platforms (pluginval passes), the audit shows 0 FAIL, and `CHANGELOG.md` is updated.
Hakan is on Windows 11 with Ableton Live 12, so the Windows VST3 artifact is the one he loads.
Never change `PLUGIN_MANUFACTURER_CODE`/`PLUGIN_CODE` (or the bundle id, or the APVTS state type `"viber"`): hosts identify the plug-in by them, and saved sessions would lose it or its settings.
